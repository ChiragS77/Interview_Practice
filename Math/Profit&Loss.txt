🟦 4. Finding Profit% or Loss%
Given: CP and SP → Use formula:

\text{Profit%} = \frac{Profit}{CP} \times 100 \text{Loss%} = \frac{Loss}{CP} \times 100

🟨 9. Equal Selling Price, Different CP

\text{Overall Loss%} = \frac{\text{Common %}^2}{100}
